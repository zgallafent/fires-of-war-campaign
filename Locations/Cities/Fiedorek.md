Fiedorek is an island and town on the coast of [[Skjoldhelm]]. The volcano has served as a place of significance for thousands of years. It is considered a sacred site by the dwarves and has been a worship site for various gods throughout history and holds a lot of religious significance for many groups. In more recent history the dwarves turned it into a Straag, or fortress. Over time various other races moved in until it became a mix of many different groups. In the early 3rd century [[Nova Aetas|NA]], the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]]  conquered it. Since then they have driven out the non-human races.

In 438•NA, the Anachreon discovered the ruins of an ancient city that had been destroyed and buried. On the 02•AL•441•NA, an [[Arcaneum]] archaeological team led by [[Aetheleron]] arrived. The digsite remained active until the 23•IN•447•NA. During this time they discovered the [[Last Saga of The Izuri]], various murals of ancient conquests, and a [[Izuri Gate System|gate]] made of [[fiedorum]] buried even further below. The gate is 15 feet tall and 8 feet wide. It was damaged when it was buried, but the material has sparked a fervor of research.
