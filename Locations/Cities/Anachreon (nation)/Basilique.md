Basilique is the largest [[Anachreonan Education#Academy|academy]] city in the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]] and the second largest city of the [[Creon Coast]]. Its situated in a bay near the eastern edge of the [[Thalassian Coast]], which makes it a major trading hub as the last port city until [[Mystara]]. It also serves as the administrative capitol of the [[Cindervale]].

# Academy Cindervale
Academy Cindervale takes students from the Cindervale, [[Thalassian Highlands]], and occasionally the [[Midlands]]. It used to be the largest academy in the Anachreon. However, [[Lumier#Academy Cryss|Academy Cryss]] became larger after it absorbed the displaced students from [[Wicksbend#Academy Uczkara|Academy Uczkara]] after it was destroyed.
