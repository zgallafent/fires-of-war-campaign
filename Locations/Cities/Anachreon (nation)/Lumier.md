Lumier is the third largest city in the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]] and the second largest [[Anachreonan Education#Academy|academy]] city. It's located on the [[Old West Road]], near where the [[Cryss River]] feeds into the [[Mornewelle River|Mornwelle]]. It serves as the administrative capitol for the Cryss river basin.
# Academy Cryss
Academy Cryss takes students from the area around the [[Cryss River]], [[Skjoldhelm]], and around [[Dornquar]]. Additionally, since the destruction of [[Wicksbend#Academy Uczkara|Academy Uczkara]], it takes students from [[Uczkara]] as well, which has allowed it to surpass [[Basilique#Academy Cindervale|Academy Cindervale]] as the largest academy.
