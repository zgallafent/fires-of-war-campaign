Harthal Cross is a trade town at the intersection of the [[Aldan River]] and the [[Old West Road]] in the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]], near the border of the [[Vichon Order]]. It's seven days travel west of [[Wycrof]].

The foundation of the town is an ancient dwarven bridge that crosses the Aldan. Buildings cover the top and sides of the bridge. There are eight main columns to the bridge, which people use as a way to travel up and down to piers built around the columns at water level. This makes the space around the columns on the bridge more valuable.

The town is known for having a lot of crime. Everyone in the town openly carries at least a knife as a form of common sense protection.

The Anachreon recently established a small garrison of a dozen soldiers to watch for soldiers from the [[Iron Consortium]]. The soldiers don't do any policing in the town. Recently the city has established a militia to deal with plague victims coming up from the south and collect a toll from travelers approaching the town. However, recently several members of the militia were [[15 - The West Road Rough|killed]] by a group of travelers.
