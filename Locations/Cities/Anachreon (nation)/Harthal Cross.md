Harthal Cross is a trade town at the intersection of the [[Aldan River]] and the [[Old West Road]] in the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]], near the border of the [[Vichon Order]]. It's seven days travel west of [[Wycrof]].

The foundation of the town is an ancient dwarven bridge that crosses the Aldan. Buildings cover the top and sides of the bridge.

The town is known for having a lot of crime.

The Anachreon recently established a small garrison of a dozen soldiers to watch for soldiers from the [[Iron Consortium]]. The soldiers don't do any policing in the town.
