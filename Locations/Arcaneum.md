The Arcaneum is a massive magical university at the heart of the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]]. Originally a research library and arcanum conservatory in the [[Izure|Izure empire]], after their fall the city of [[Anachreon (city)|Anachreon]] grew around it.

The Arcaneum accepts generally only accepts students that have completed 10 years of study at a smaller academy in the Anachreon. Students then require another 10 years of study at the Arcaneum to graduate. Professors will select some of those students to stay at the Arcaneum as research assistants, while the rest are sent out to work throughout the Anachreon.
