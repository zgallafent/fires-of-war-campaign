The Aldan River is a river in the [[Midlands]] region of western [[Anchara]]. It flows south from the [[Gaelica|Gaelican Range]] south to the [[Mornewelle River]], feeding into it just as the Mornwelle bends west to leave the [[Lynocean Plain]].

One of its tributaries is the [[Lhune River]], which has a small town where the two meet.
