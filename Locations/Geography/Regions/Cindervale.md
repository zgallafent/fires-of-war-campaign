The Cindervale is a administrative and geographical region surrounding the Cindervale Mountains in the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]]. It's bordered by the [[Thalassian Highlands]] to the west and the [[Midlands]] to the south. [[Basilique]] is the capitol of the region. [[Wycrof]] is also a major trade hub within it.
