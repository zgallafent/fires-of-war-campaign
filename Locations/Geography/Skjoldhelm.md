Skjoldhelm is an island in the [[Vestmar]], north of [[Anchara]]. Its mostly inhabited by various human and half-giant tribes (Trevor described as similar to Gaelic/German tribes in Roman times). There's no good estimates for how many people live in these tribes. The [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]] has established some presence on the southern coast, but has made little progress in expanding because of the fierce independence of the tribes and their dislike of the Arcaneum.
