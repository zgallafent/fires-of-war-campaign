Sahuagin (/sɑːˈhuːɑːgɪn/) are a race of fish-like humanoids from the warm southern seas of [[Anchara]]. They have their own language. They may be led by religious leaders, possibly priestesses.

They generally hunt easy prey such as injured animals or stray children. They ride sharks in the ocean. Flocks of birds may follow them as they hunt, which can be a sign that they're approaching.

While normally sahuagin would stay to their territories in the southern seas, they've been pushed out and are roaming further north. They describe a "buzz" in their bones that makes their young sick and originates somewhere in the south.
