N'duka is an orc captain from the so western orc lands. He regularly travels north to trade in [[Mystara]] and to hunt west of [[Straag Bohumin]].


## Relationship
### Affection
**3/6**
I've only ever known N'duka as our employer. While he gets along well enough with everyone and seems to have a friendship of sorts with [[Ghithgod]], so far the extent of my interactions with him have generally just been transactional.
### Respect
**5/6**
N'duka knows many things about many places I've never been. I could spend hours asking him about the lands south of the [[Iron Consortium]].

In our [[13 - The Sahuagin Strike|battle]] with the [[Sahuagin|sahuagin]], N'duka took nearly as many blows as Ghithgod did but barely seemed phased by it. He's very clearly a deadly fighter that I wouldn't want to be on the wrong side of 
### Trust
**5/6**
N'duka has always been very frank with us and doesn't try to keep any information to himself. That's something I rarely ever saw in the [[Arcaneum]] and I respect him a lot for it.

When a sahuagin swordsman nearly managed to kill me, N'duka and his first mate stepped in immediately to intervene. He obviously cares a lot for his crew, but he seemed equally concerned with us as well even though we were hired hands.
