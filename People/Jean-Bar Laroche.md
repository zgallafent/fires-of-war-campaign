Jean-Bar Laroche is a man in [[Harthal Cross]]. He owns the Wayside Tavern, a popular tavern that sits cantilevered over one of the main bridge columns. One of his eyes is ruined from an old scar. 

Jean-Bar is a very influential man in the city. He never forgets a name or a face and has a way of knowing everything that happens in or around the city. This allows him to be a powerful information broker.
