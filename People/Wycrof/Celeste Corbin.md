Celeste Corbin is a human woman they lives at [[Les Fréres Corbin Vineyard]] near [[Wycrof]].

She is significantly involved in the day-to-day operations of the vineyard.

She is a member of the [[Ember Veil]]. She helps the organization by working as an information broker, trading favors, and connecting people.

Her father is Marceaux Corbin.

On 12.IM.463.[[Nova Aetas|NA]], she [[15 - The West Road Rough|arranged]] help for a band of adventurers to travel into [[Iron Consortium|Consortium]] territory in exchange for the promise of a future favor.
