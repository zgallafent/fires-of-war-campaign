Narnod is a rock gnome from [[Zemvalda]]. He was born in [[Leovolan]] on the 1st of Imperium, 413 [[Nova Aetas|NA]]. He moved to [[Wicksbend]] and lived with his cousin [[Hazelmere|Hazelmere's]] family to study at the local academy from 414 NA to 424 NA. After he finished, he moved to [[Tirnavis]] to work. He maintained a close relationship with Hazelmere through letters for the next five years, until Wicksbend was destroyed. While the two have continued to send letters occasionally over the years since then, Hazelmere's constant travels have made steady communication difficult.
