Ajon Bilius is a [[Vichon Order|Vichian]] halfling. He lives in a cottage on the outskirts of Catuprenna.

Ajon served the last king of the Vichon Order decades ago as the chief cartographer, which has resulted in him being known as the Mapmaker. When the king fell, he carried off many of their records in secret and went into hiding in the [[Midlands]] near Catuprenna.

When the [[The Hunger#Wendigo|Wendigo]] came to ravage Catuprenna, he and [[Decimus Corvin]] were the only survivors left in the town. A [[18 - Wendigo|group of adventures]], sent by [[Caroline Harkus]], found him there in their search for the [[Izuri Gate System|Izuri Gates]].
