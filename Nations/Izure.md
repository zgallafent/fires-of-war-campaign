The Izure (more commonly known as the Old Ones, Ancient Ones, or Ancients) was an empire that controlled all of [[Anchara]]. They spread their influence across the region using the [[Izuri Gate System]], a series of arcane gates that could transport people between them instantaneously. They were wiped out around 2,100 years ago.
## The Fall of Izure
The fall of Izure was a cataclysmic event that wiped out the entire empire in a very short period of time. The [[Last Saga of the Izuri]] describes it as a "terrible storm", which shook the ground and rent the air. The storm destroyed entire cities and supposedly cursed the land for a thousand years.

## Modern Views
While few people know much of their their history, many people know that they existed. All major cities are built on the ruins of ancient Izuri cities, and many major buildings are set directly on the surviving foundations.

The Izure is typically described in legends. These are most often warning fables and ghost stories.
