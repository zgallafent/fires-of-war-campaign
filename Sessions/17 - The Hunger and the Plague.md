The night after our [[16 - The War-Torn Aldan|battle]] with the river bandits, [[Ghithgod]] had a nightmare of the plague. He saw it killing his father, his sister [[Nadar]], and his friend. He relived burning the boat of plague victims while they were all on board.

We continued sailing south along the [[Aldan River]] for several more days, collecting the various caches that had been marked by the bandits along the river. We also found a map in the bandits' notes that had many locations marked warning of something called "Wendigo." We talked to [[Aedric]] and [[Henric]] about what it was. Aedric said they had stories about The Hunger in the [[Highlands]], but he thought they were only stories to teach people to be good to each other, not travel alone, and not let each other go hungry. He repeated an old adage, "The Hunger goes behind you. To fear it is to give it power. To name it is to make it."

Henric told us the stories of the Wendigo in the [[Midlands]], which he claimed was the name of The Hunger. According to the stories, as villages ran out of food and the people began to starve, they would start hearing something walking the streets at night. First collection bowls were moved, then there would be a human tooth in one, then people started disappearing and the villagers would find meat in their bowls in the morning. On the coldest night of a year, something would go knocking door to door asking if they were hungry. He said he didn't know if the stories were true, just that there were many empty villages. Collins asked if there were any signs of a beast in the villages like claw marks. Henric repeated claims of bodies that had been found eaten, with marks that didn't match any known animal.

On another day, [[Collins de Jarrag Iolaire|Collins]] asked Ghithgod about the ritualistic knot tying that he does and if it was a practice associated with any specific god. Ghithgod told us that it's called the [[Binding Way]], an ancient druidic practice he had learned that helps connect him to the world. [[Marlamin]] said that there are two different kinds of power in the world: the divine and the weave. Clerics worked divine power through their worship of gods or devils, while druids and wizards got their power by respectively connecting to or manipulating the weave.

On our seventh day on the river, we pass a small travellers camp on the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]] side of the river. A little further on, we found the bodies of a dozen dwarves that had been killed and hung up in the trees as a warning to the [[Iron Consortium]] about crossing the river.

We finally left the barge on the eighth day, disembarking just downstream of a small town. Henric warned us to be careful and asked who he should have come after us if we don't return. Aedric said nobody would come after us anyways, but Henric still promised to tell [[Jean-Bar Laroche|Laroche]].

As we reached the first day of our trek inland, we found a shrine to the [[Pelor|Dawnfather]], hundreds of years old. We camped for the night near it, and throughout the night we felt an uneasy, oppressive silence.

We ended the next day near an ancient [[Izure|Izuri]] ruin. It had been defaced as if someone was trying to remove all signs of the [[Izure]], but [[Hazelmere]] still spent some time sketching it. That night, Marlamin heard during his watch a mournful howling on the wind. When Collins woke up to take his turn, he found Marlamin over by the treeline standing in front of some bushes. When Collins approached him, Marlamin startled as if he had been sleep. We all agreed that we'd always have two people awake for each watch to be safe.

The next day started with rain and the smell of smoke on the wind. We eventually came across an old Binding Way, which looked to have been added to a lot recently. A little further on we found an abandoned town. At the center were three giant bonfires that had been used to burn bodies; they looked to have last been tended to a day ago.

That evening, Aedric told us we should reach [[Catuprenna]] the next day. We talked a bit about what had happened to that last town we passed through, and Hazelmere and Aedric had a small argument about what could have prevented all of these people from dying. Aedric blamed all of the countries for abandoning the people, but mostly the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]]. Hazelmere said that the Anachreon could have taught these people to heal themselves so the plague didn't come for then when the healers left.

Aedric asked us where we had come from and Hazelmere said he was from [[Wicksbend]] in the Anachreon. Aedric asked if Hazelmere still worked for the Anachreon and he said no, but that he still values its foundations. Aedric then said he figured the rest of us were from the Iron Consortium because of our accents and Marlamin said specifically [[Straag Huldar]]. Aedric asked how we had traveled so far and Hazelmere said we couldn't tell him, but that we had been working with [[Ghilain|Brother Ghilain]].

Aedric told us about how, when his family refused to allow his brother to be recruited for the war, soldiers burned down their farm and took his brother anyways. After that, he found his way into joining the [[Ember Veil]] and working for [[Celeste Corbin]]. He said that she gave people jobs to see what they're made of and would always take care of her own. 

That night, Ghithgod and Hazelmere were on watch together. Ghithgod asked how Hazelmere felt towards the Anachreon and if he work for them again. Hazelmere answered that he felt like the Anachreon had done a lot of good for people and we shouldn't lose that, even if there needed to be changes. Hazelmere asked about Ghithgod's feelings towards the Consortium in kind, but as Ghithgod began to answer, Hazelmere went into some kind of trance. When Ghithgod grabbed him to get his attention, Hazelmere said he couldn't remember what happened. 

We reached Catuprenna the next day. The town was completely empty and we could feel a weight in the air as we entered it. Aedric warned us to be quiet and there was something up ahead. We heard a baleful moan resonate through the town and Marlamin heard a voice say to him, "I'm hungry." We then saw the Wendigo come out from behind a building. It looked directly at Marlamin and called out his name.
