In a cabin high up in the [[Kharazul Pass]], [[Ghithgod]] sat beside a fire, reclining on furs as he practiced the somatics of a spell. [[Bhalish]], sat nearby on a wooden stool. He was alternating between writing on some paper with an ink pen, stirring a pot on the fire, and watching Ghithgod practice. Eventually, he said, "You keep pushing, Ghithgod. How does it feel?"

Ghithgod replied that it was difficult. Bhalish encouraged him, saying that he needed to guide the magic instead of forcing it. Ghithgod tried the spell again and grew frustrated as it failed, but under Bhalish's finally managed to conjure a partial bloom.

Bhalish commended Ghithgod on his progress, then offered him some of the food he was preparing. Then, Bhalish told Ghithgod that "she" is always there to help him and that "it" is almost ready.

Ghithgod woke up from that dream on [[N'duka|N'duka's]] ship, which had been recently [[12 - Stormy Seas|beached]] for repairs. It was evening and the storm has passed on to the southern horizon. The half-dozen members of N'duka's crew were unloading spoiled cargo and doing repairs on the hull of the ship and the mainsail. N'duka told us that we would need to stay at least one night on shore to get the ship in good enough shape to sail back to [[Mystara]].

The first mate called N'duka to the stern and pointed out to sea, where we could see a flock of birds approaching from overhead. Something churned in the water beneath the flock. As they got close, N'duka called out for the crew to draw steel. We quickly prepared ourselves for a fight, and then a group of fishlike humanoids burst out of the water. They had surrounded the ship riding on the back of sharks and climbed up the side of the boat onto the deck.

Fighting broke out all across the ship as we and the crew engaged the creatures, which the crew called [[Sahuagin|sahuagin]]. Six of the sahuagin fought with claws, two used spears, two had pairs of swords that they were very skilled with. One swordsman jumped straight on [[Hazelmere]] and nearly killed him, while the other engaged with Ghithgod and [[Collins de Jarrag Iolaire|Collins]]. The other sahuagin began fighting with the crew. [[Marlamin]], N'duka, his first mate , and Hazelmere's defender stepped in to save Hazelmere as he escaped. The first mate called out, "They're here for the ship! Leave none of them alive!"

As the fighting broke out, two more sahuagin climbed on deck at the center of the ship. They were covered in ceremonial jewelry made of teeth and bones and began conjuring ethereal tridents and blasts of holy energy to attack us. Hazelmere used his crossbow to harry them, breaking their concentration so the tridents evaporated. Collins and Ghithgod worked together to cut down one of the sahuagin swordsmen, nearly killing them, though, Ghithgod took heavy blows in exchange. Marlamin healed Hazelmere and Ghithgod to keep them in the fight, but the other swordsman managed to kill the first mate. The crew were managing to push back the rest of the sahuagin, but only barely. 

N'duka stabbed the swordsman he was fighting and then pulled out a hand crossbow to shoot them in the chest, nearly killing them. The swordsman cried something out in an unknown language, looking to the sahuagin priestesses in fear. N'duka called out that they were scared and to keep pushing. Collins realized that the priestesses were the leaders of this band of sahuagin and broke off from the swordsman to attack one, severely wounding. It let out a cry and the sahuagin began to break ranks, with the priestess and several others diving into the sea.

The swordsman that had been fighting Ghithgod chased after Collins to protect the priestess, but Ghithgod cut them down and killed them as they turned away, then struck down a few other sahuagin before they could escape. N'duka knocked the other swordsman unconscious with the butt of his crossbow. Marlamin blasted the remaining priestess with holy energy and Hazelmere shot them with his crossbow, killing them. Most of the other sahuagin escaped back into the sea, though they managed to kill off one of the crew in the final exchange.

After the fight ended, the crew began throwing the dead sahuagin bodies off the deck into the water. N'duka was barely injured, but he stood mournful over the body of his dead first mate as a crew member comforted him. The crew member asked what could have driven them so far north and N'duka explained to us that sahuagin were native to the warm seas far south of here. He said they had recently seemed disturbed, as if something were driving them out of their territories. They also normally only went after wounded animals or stray children, so he thought they must have been desperate to attack a full crew. The crewmate asked if maybe it was because of the dark creatures they had been seeing in the waters to the south.

When we asked what they meant, N'duka explained that starting a decade ago rumors had started spreading of abomination killing people, or sometimes entire ships. He said at first they seemed like just embellished sailors tales of creatures driving people mad or draining them of their blood, but that they'd been growing more frequent over the last few years. They seemed to originate from an island in the sea near the orc homeland, but nobody knew which one. Hazelmere said that they heard of similar things in the Anachreon and that we might be able to help if they could identify the particular island. N'duka replied that they'd have to ask around to find out, but would appreciate the help to deal with it, as it was poisoning their waters and killing the fish. 

N'duka tied the unconscious sahuagin to the remaining mast and asked us to heal him to ensure he would survive his injuries. Over the next few days sailed back to [[Mystara]], during which N'duka interrogated the sahuagin. He eventually told us that the sahuagin had been feeling a "buzz" in their bones, which was stronger the further south they were. It was making their young sick and driving them north. The sahuagin was gone before we reached port. The crew also performed some ceremonies for their dead and preserved them with herbal medicines so they could take the bodies back home. Once we arrived in port, N'duka thanked us for saving his crew and paid us each 85 gold pieces. He said that they would stay in port for another day to collect their whaling profits and making repairs before returning south.
