Two days after our [[13 - The Sahuagin Strike|battle]] with the [[Sahuagin|sahuagin]], [[N'duka]] gathered the crew on deck to hold a ceremony for their fallen. They pounded on their chests and the deck to create rhythmic beats as N'duka led them in a call-and-response chant in orcish. During the ceremony, [[Marlamin]] looked into one of the braziers burning on deck and saw the golden hair of [[Tymora]] flowing out of it. It stretched out into the sky and intertwined with the path of the gods far above us. Marlamin heard a voice whisper in his ear, "What are you looking for?" He looked around and saw nobody near him.

The vision took Marlamin's sight to a rocky island in the far south, with darkness spilling out of it. He was then carried to a foreign bustling city. He then saw the ruined fortress of [[Bridgewater]], then the tower on the outskirts of [[Wycrof]], then many other places passing in a blur. Marlamin had a strong sense all of these places were connected, and that they were waking up. He could feel a great, malicious darkness arousing itself far below. He felt a deep buzzing in his bones as it grew in strength, until it was impossible to bear. He then woke up, collapsed in [[Collins de Jarrag Iolaire|Collins]] arms. As Collins pulled Marlamin to his feet, he heard the same voice say, "The pathway will lead you to where you must go."

The next day, Marlamin brought us all together during lunch to tell us about what he saw. He asked [[Hazelmere]] if it was possible that the creatures we had [[06 - Hags in Hiding|seen]] and heard about were coming from another plane. Marlamin especially wondered if they could have come from the Shadowfell or Feywild. Hazelmere explained to us the the wheel of the planes and how they related to the Material Plane, as well as how it was connected to the two Marlamin mentioned. He said that the darkness Marlamin saw in his vision sounded like something from the Abyss, but he couldn't be sure. Marlamin suggested that, if the [[Heavens Gate]] was supposed to connect to one of the Good planes, it couldn't exist without a gate existing on the opposite Evil plane as well.

We all discussed whether a plane to another realm should exist. We all agreed that we should stop the gates from connecting to an evil realm like the Abyss if possible, but we disagreed about what to do if a gate could truly take us to a Good plane. Hazelmere argued that it would allow more true Good to flow into the Material Plane, while Marlamin thought a direct connection to the divine would be too dangerous. 

The final day before we arrived in [[Mystara]], N'duka approached [[Ghithgod]]. He said that he appreciated the work all of us had done, but most especially Ghithgod. N'duka and the crew wanted to present Ghithgod with a gift and led him below deck. He brought Ghithgod into the mess hall, where the entire crew was waiting, and offered to tattoo on him the mark of their crew. Ghithgod accepted the offer. Over the next day, a rotating cast of crewmembers came by to work and marking Ghithgod over the heart with the tattoo, as well as singing songs and sharing stories.

After we arrived, we bid N'duka farewell and went to the [[Kithenir|kithenir]] to meet with [[Caroline Harkus|Harkus]]. We told her about the sahuagin we fought and the rumors about darkness and monsters among the southern isles. We suggested there might be a gate there. Marlamin asked her about the possibility of a gate on another plane and she explained to us more details about how the magic of teleportation worked and what that might mean for an interplanar gate, but didn't say whether one might exist. She told us that the Kithenir were packing up to leave, so we would need to as well.

Harkus recommended that we connect with the [[Ember Veil]] again in [[Wycrof]] to find a guide that could take us south into the [[Midlands]]. She said we should then try to find [[Ajon Billius]], a cartographer and old friend of hers, in [[Catuprenna]]. Hazelmere suggested we sail south with N'duka's crew to the [[Bohuminkaraz River|Bohuminkaraz]] and walk east to avoid travelling in [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]] territory, but Harkus said it would be too slow compared to sailing south on the [[Aldan River|Aldan]] from [[Harthal Cross]] and disembarking before [[Dornquar]].

Once we agreed to her plan, Harkus told us that we could pick up Collins's swords from [[Zaiha]] and that she had helped to enchant them. When we left the Kithenir, Hazelmere split off to go to the docks while we went to to Zaiha. She was in her home eating dinner, but when she saw Collins she got became excited and led us into her smithy. Zaiha pulled out two swords, handed one to Collins, then said he'd have to prove himself to keep them and attacked him with the other. Marlamin quickly cast a blessing on Collins, but otherwise he and Ghithgod didn't interfere as they sparred.  Once she was satisfied, she have the other sword to Collins and they settled the debt. 

After we left the smithy, Marlamin and Collins asked to make a detour to the [[Wayfarer's Rest]], where we met with [[Lorea]]. Marlamin asked her if they had a symbol of [[Tymora]] he could take with him as a memento of this place. She said she has a dream that it was time for her to pass on her old holy symbol and gave it to him. We started to leave, but Collins went back and talk to Lorea alone. He said she once told him that Tymora doesn't force a path on people, then asked if she ever does give someone a path. Lorea replied that Tymora sometimes does choose a champion, someone that can serve as a caretaker of the path. However, her influence on us is very limited because there's a barrier between us and the gods that's only somewhat permeable. Additionally, Lorea said that Tymora sees choice as sacred and often comes into conflict with other gods to defend it. Collins asked what color Tymora's hair would be if he saw her and Lorea answered that it would depend on what he saw.

While we were talking to Zaiha and Lorea, Hazelmere went down to the docks to find. Hazelmere gave N'duka an enchanted sending stone, which he said would last for 100 days. He said that we had seen similar monsters to the ones that the crew had described among the isles to the south and he thought he knew how to stop them. He asked N'duka to try and find out the island where the darkness was originating from and then to send him a message through the sending stone. N'duka was confused about the stone and how it would work, but he said he'd ask around and see what he could find out.

We met back up at the Kithenir, where we spent one last night before leaving for Wycrof. As Harkus was sending us through the teleportation circle, she told us to not seek her out and that she'd find us. After being teleported, [[Osbert Harkus]] opened the trapdoor to the cellar for us and let us out. Before we left, Hazelmere bought a night vision elixir from him and Marlamin nearly bought an elixir of vigour, but eventually changed his mind.

We stepped out of the shop and decided to go to [[Agathe Brelin]] instead of [[Ghilain|Brother Ghilain]] to ask about a guide. When we met her in the [[Laurel Crown Inn]], she took us up into a small cupboard-turned-office and asked what we needed. She told us not to give her too many details, but we eventually agreed to buy two weeks of rations from her and she directed us to [[Celeste Corbin]] at [[Les Frères Corbin]] for a guide. We headed west out of town, passing by an old abandoned halfling village on the way.

When we arrived at the vineyard, a servant greeted us. We asked to see Celeste Corbin and the servant had us wait in a side room for a while. Eventually, she came to meet us and asked if we had some kind of referral. We told her Agathe Brelin had sent us to her for help and she took us into a private study to talk more.
