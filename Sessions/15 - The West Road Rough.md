Behind a mill at [[Collins de Jarrag Iolaire|Collins]]'s childhood home, Collins sparred against his tutor [[Garrick]] with wooden swords. He baited Collins forward and then suddenly lunges, striking him with his sword. Garrick said that Collins always falls for the feints because he's angry and asked why he is. Collins answered that he's not angry, just nervous and excited for his plan. He said that if it goes well, everything will be well. As they continue sparring, Garrick commented that we often attack most when we feel small. Collins said he does feel small, but his plan will impress the visiting nobles so that their house isn't small anymore. He wanted to show the other houses that they can trust House Jarrag.

Lord Andoran was expected to arrive for a visit soon and Garrick said that Collins's father was afraid of what might happen. He replied that they didn't need to feel small because their house was already exceeding any expectations for them. As they finished their training, Garrick's final comment was that Collins had become a dangerous swordsman, but someday would come across something he couldn't overcome on his own. Garrick asked him, when that time can't, to not be too proud to ask for help. 

After [[Celeste Corbin]] [[14 - Back to Mystara, Back to Wycrof|brought us]]
into an office, we made our introductions. She said she'd heard of us and the things we'd done for the people of the [[Cindervale]]. She explained that she's an information broker and helps people with information and connections in these dangerous times. We explained that we were trying to reach the [[Midlands]] by heading south on the [[Aldan River|Aldan]] from [[Harthal Cross]]. While she's thankful for what we've done so far, she acknowledged that we've already been compensated for it and we'd owe her a favor in the future for her help. We agreed and she said that she could introduce to a man that worked in trade and transportation that would arrive later that day. 

Celeste invited us to have lunch with her and called in a servant to bring some food. As we ate, she acknowledged that [[Hazelmere]] is from the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]] and asked where the rest of us came from. [[Marlamin]] immediately answered that he he's not Anachreonan and hates them. Celeste said she understands how he feels and that she wished her people were free. Hazelmere said that they wanted the [[Wycrof]] to be like it was before the [[Banner Wars]]. She replied that nobody remembered what that was like, but that they didn't want to live under oppressors or be forced to fight unjust wars. Celeste asked about the holy symbol Marlamin was wearing and he explained that he had been a devout of [[Tymora]] for many years. She commented him for his faith, but warned that wearing it so openly could bring trouble. When Collins asked why, she and Hazelmere explained that devotion to the gods is discouraged in the Anachreon.

After we finished our meal, Celeste invited us to rest here for the rest of the day and to leave with her contact in the morning. As we waited, Hazelmere approached Collins privately and asked how long Marlamin had actually worshipped Tymora. He was worried that Marlamin's fervour had suddenly risen after his resurrection. Collins answered that Marlamin had always been religious, but it had stepped up in intensity recently. However, as long as it wasn't leading Marlamin off the path, he wasn't worried yet. Hazelmere said he was worried that the description Marlamin gave of Tymora didn't match his she was classically described because it could be someone impersonating her. Collins asked who that could be and if it was another god. Hazelmere said that it could be, or possibly a devil or demon. Collins then asked Hazelmere if he could identify any hidden meanings or attributes in the runes inscribed on his sword, but Hazelmere couldn't find any. 

In the evening, the building began to fill up as workers came in from the fields and traders arrived in wagons. It was the last harvest of the season, so it was far busier than usual. A man named [[Aedric]] approached us and said Celeste had told him we needed his help. When we explained where we were going, he said that it would be dangerous but that he could get us there. Hazelmere asked how long it would take and he said a minimum of 15-20 days. Aedric also said that we would need to handle our own supplies, as well as a barge from Harthal Cross, which Hazelmere said we'd be and to do. He told us that Harthal Cross is about a seven day journey from here and that we'd leave in the morning. Collins asked what kind of trouble we could expect to run into and Aedric replied that the area we'd be traveling through is hotly contested territory.

That evening was a feast to celebrate the end of the harvest, with celebrations lasting until midnight. Celeste assigned her servant Hannah to help us around until we left and she showed us to the bunks in the communal room we'd be staying in for the night. 

In the morning, we had a quick breakfast and began loading into Aedric' wagon. During that time, Hazelmere asked Hannah to bring him to Celeste. When they found her, he asked Celeste if he could get another favor if she could get some money delivered to a his mother [[Lamara]] in [[Mazrunas]]. He said he took her 50 gold every season for living expenses, but with how long the trip would be he wouldn't be back for winter in time. She agreed if he would be able to pay the 15 gold for a messenger to travel that far. She also asked him for a letter they could give to any friend Hazelmere might have in the Anachreon Arcaneum that could help them get into Mazrunas. He gave her the money and a letter addressed to [[Erik Skjoldson]].

We headed west into heavy rains for the first three days of travel. We didn't have adequate gear to keep us warm in the storms and ended up making poor progress those first few days, as well as half of our rations spoiling. However, the rain cleared up by the fourth day and we ended up making back the lost time. We made it to the outskirts of Harthal Cross on the sixth day and camped with the town in view.

That evening, as we sat around the fire together, Aedric told us a bit of what to expect. He warned us that, while it's a good place, it's also lawless and we'd to watch our backs and purses. He then idly sang a song as we stared up at the stars.

After he finished singing, we heard a man clapping from beyond the firelight. He approached us and said that we'd missed paying the toll on the road. Two more men approached from the opposite side of the fire. The leader said they'd come to collect, then the fighting began.

In total, seven bandits and one hound came into the firelight to attack us. Two had climbed up into the trees while we were unaware, from which they threw a few nets. The hound and two bandits, one with a scimitat and the other a crossbow and the hound come up from around the wagon. The leader, armed with two scimitars, and the final bandit approached from behind Aedric.

Collins reacted first and rushed the bandits by the wagon, nearly killing one in his initial flurry. He avoided the net thrown at him plus several crossbow bolts, but was bit by the hound. Hazelmere's steel defender ran up to attack the hound and missed, but gave Collins an opening to cut its head off. The two bandits he's fighting fled, too terrified of Collins to keep fighting.

The second net managed to entangle Marlamin, but Ghithgod tore it apart before turning to helping Aedric. The leader and the bandit focused on Aedric and heavily wounded him, but Hazelmere healed him and they were able to land a few good blows in retaliation.

Marlamin fired a guiding bolt up into a tree and killed one of the bandits. The other jumped down to attack Marlamin, but he cast inflict wounds on the bandit and Collins ran in to finish them off. The leader called for the bandits to retreat and took off running. Marlamin managed to hit him as they fled with another guiding bolt, but they escaped. Ghithgod and Aedric finally managed to kill the last bandit.

After the fight ended, Aedric settled down the horses while we collected any valuables they had, then buried them in a shallow grave. Collins carved a sign that said "Tolltaker" and planted it in the grave, then we finally settled in to rest for the night.
