The morning after we were [[15 - The West Road Rough|ambushed]], we walked the final few miles to [[Harthal Cross]]. On the way there, [[Marlamin]] had a vision of approaching the town as an old man, long ago when the bridge was only a few centuries old. At the time, it was just a burgeoning fishing village. He's come to spread word of the [[Tymora|Changebringer]].

[[Aedric]] warned us before we arrived that we should watch our backs and our purses while in the city. He also said we should take care to be respectful, since the only kind of law in the city were relationships. He had a friend at the [[Wayside Tavern]] that he said could help us out, though we'd need to provide 125 gold to cover the supplies, barge, and stable for his cart and horses. Collins also asked him to try and get ahold of a fishing rod and bait.

At the tavern, we met [[Jean-Bar Laroche]]. He went into a private room with Aedric while we sat down for breakfast. As we were eating, a man rushed into the tavern yelling that Amon's crew had been attacked. As the people in the tavern discussed what had happened, we realized that the group we thought were bandits last night was actually the town militia. Aedric soon came into the room and hurried us out the back door of the tavern, saying we needed to get out of town before anybody realized we were the ones that killed the militiamen.

We went down a small lift to the base of the bridge column, where a small barge was being loaded with supplies. [[Henric]], the captain, quickly put us to work and after the last supplies were in board we set sail south. 

That first day, we came across a riverboat drifting against the bank with a small flag tied to the mast. We saw several desperately ill people on the boat, as well as many more bodies. As we approached, [[Marlamin]] held up his holy symbol to bless the people on the boat and ease their pain. Henric said they were plague victims from further south and that he hoped none of them would make it off the boat for it to spread  [[Collins de Jarrag Iolaire]] pulled a flask of oil and wrapped an oil cloth around an arrow, planning to burn the boat to make sure none did. [[Hazelmere]] had his steel defender take the oil flask and carry it over to the boat, at which point Collins shot it with his flaming arrow to set the ship on fire. The cries of the people on board severely affected [[Ghithgod]] and [[Collins de Jarrag Iolaire|Collins]], forcing them to go below deck to get away.

The next day, a militia boat pulled up alongside us. They appeared because to approach, called for us to let them come aboard. Henric warned they'd want to see some kind of documentation and that his wouldn't cover us. We tons Aedric about the cover story we'd used previously, that Hazelmere was headed to the [[Vichon Order]] for research and we were protecting him. When the militia boarded the barge, Aedric and Hazelmere tried to convince their captain of it, but he was suspicious of us because of how visibly nervous Ghithgod and Marlamin were. Aedric palmed the man 30 gold as a bribe, then when they left told Ghithgod and Marlamin they needed to pay him back. 

We had heavy rain throughout the entirety of the next day, leaving us cold and miserable but otherwise no worse for wear. The day after, we started seeing dead animals floating on the river and fish rotting on the shore. We can't tell what the cause is or if it's related to the plague. 

The next day, we see a small family out fishing on a boat. They run a river house that used to see a lot of travelers, but hasn't had anyone stop in for weeks. We agree to spend the night there, allowing us to get our first hot meal in a few days. 

Late in the day after we left the river house, we hear a series of splashes as a chain is pulled up in front of the barge. As it stopped us in the middle of the river, a system of pullies quickly dragged three boats from the banks of the river over to our barge. There were eight bandits on the boats and they called out to us to give up our cargo or they'd kill us.

As the boats approached, Hazelmere shot a dwarf in one of the boats with his crossbow and nearly killed them. The bandits responded by firing their crossbows and bows at us, managing to hit Marlamin. Aedric, Collins, and Henric fired a few shots as well, but only Collins managed to hit anyone Marlamin summoned a spiritual weapon to start attacking the bandits. Ghithgod ran to the edge of the boat and hurled his trident at the bandits' leader, hitting them in the chest and knocking them off the boat. The leader managed to catch the edge of the boat as they fell and pulled themself back on board just as the boats reached the barge.

The boats took the bandits to the lower deck at the front of the barge, so as they started climbing aboard Hazelmere threw a flask of grease in front of the stairs to the main deck. Collins began fighting the bandits on the left side of the barge, with Aedric, Hazelmere, and Henric firing on them from a distance. Meanwhile, Ghithgod, Marlamin, and Hazelmere's steel defender fought on the right side. 

Several bandits initially tried to rush up the steps to Collins, but fell on the grease and couldn't reach him. However, the bandit leader was able to climb up the side of the barge to reach the main deck and began dueling with Collins. We and the bandits all tried to fire our boss and crossbows into the fight to help, but struggled to hit anything. Luckily Collins overpowered the bandit leader and killed him.

On the other side of the barge, two bandits climbed up the side to the main deck as well and one shot Marlamin with their crossbow. However, Ghithgod rushed over to interpose himself between the bandits and Marlamin and began brutally hacking his way through them. The bandits on the lower deck began peppering Ghithgod with arrows. However, between Marlamin casting blasts of holy magic, his spiritual weapon, Ghithgod's great sword, and the steel defender, we quickly killed the two bandits on the main deck.

After the bandit leader fell, the fighting shifted down to the lower decks. Hazelmere threw another flask of grease to disrupt the bandits as Collins, Ghithgod, and the steel defender jumped down to the lower deck. They quickly pushed back the bandits and managed to kill another one. One of the three surviving bandits called for them to flee and they ran on to one of the boats to escape. However, as they were just pushing off, Ghithgod leaped across the water onto the boat and brutally cut down two of them. The steel defender pushed the last one into the river, where they disappeared below the water.

As Henric worked to free the boat from the chains and get the barge moving again, we quickly searched the bodies for anything valuable before throwing them into the river. Besides some gold and their equipment, Hazelmere also managed to find a small book wrapped in oil cloths hidden in a secret compartment on one of the boats. It has locations marked for where the bandits had hidden caches of valuables, as well as other bits of information they had recorded about the midlands. When we went to shore to free the horses that the bandits used to run the pulleys for the boats, we also found four cases of black powder.
