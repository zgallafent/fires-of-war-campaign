The Binding Way is a druidic practice of tying symbolic knots in rope. It's used to tell stories, create blessings, or ward off danger. The chains of knots are added to over time, often across generations, and so can tell the history of the local people. 

The practice of the Binding Way is incredibly old. It's mostly practiced in the [[Vidanya]] and the [[Iron Consortium]], but there are some small areas in the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]] as well.

The core practice of the Binding Way is based on what knots are tied in the rope. However, sometimes other components like bells and clay figures are worked into the knots as well. Each chain is referred to individually as a Binding Way.
