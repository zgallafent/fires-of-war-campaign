The Hunger is a disease or psychosis that drives people to cannibalism. It's described in folktales throughout the [[Midlands]]. During times of famine, the Hunger comes to push people to cannibalism.
## The Wendigo
The Hunger spreads alongside the presence of the Wendigo. It is a tall, gaunt creature that roughly resembles a humanoid. It has an elk skull for a head and four arms.

The Wendigo is capable of basic speech in the Common tongue, but mostly communicates in howls. It's capable of influencing animals around it such as ravens and rats, causing them to swarm and fight on its behalf. When the Wendigo howls, it can inflict visions on anyone nearby of their loved ones' dying moments.

During a famine, it comes to hunt and eat humanoids. Those who are bitten by it and survive are infected with the Hunger.
