The Great Fires was a series of wildfires in 354•[[Nova Aetas|NA]] in the [[Anachreon (nation)|Anachreon]]. Reckless forest management by lumber barons caused wildfires that decimated the nation's forest. This was the catalyst for the creation of the [[Syndicate of Timber and Crown]].
